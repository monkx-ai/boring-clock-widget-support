Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add the widget to my Home Screen?

Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen, tap the + button in the top corner, search for "Boring Clock Widget," choose a size, and tap Add Widget.

How do I change the clock's look?

Open the Boring Clock Widget app, go to Customize, pick your colors and style, then tap "Save & Apply" to update your widget.

I purchased Premium but it's not unlocked.

Open the app, go to the Premium screen, and tap "Restore Purchase." If that doesn't resolve it, email us at the address above with your Apple ID purchase receipt.

Does this app collect my data?

No. Boring Clock Widget does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything runs locally on your device.
